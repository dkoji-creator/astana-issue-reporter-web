      How to report an issue

      Text version of the video
      1. Log in with your email and password.
      2. Tap the map where the problem is. A blue pin shows the chosen point.
      3. Choose a category and describe the problem in a few words.
      4. Add a photo if you have one, then press Submit Report.
      5. A green message confirms the report, and a new marker appears on the map.
